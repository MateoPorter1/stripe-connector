- Base: #f7f8f8 workspace, #ffffff panels, #22282d text, #66727a secondary text, #e3e7e9 dividers, #c7471b primary action, #fff0e8 selected navigation.
- Type: system sans for controls and data; Georgia only for page and sign-in headings. Tabular figures for balances. Avoid oversized marketing sections in the workspace.
- Layout: overview has a compact date toolbar, a dominant collected-revenue figure with supporting facts, and a split performance chart / monthly ledger. The queue has compact date controls, a full invoice workspace, and a separate sticky collection console with the explicit run action and latest status. Rules and attempt details expand on demand. Month selection opens the existing payment traceability table.
- States: green remains reserved for confirmed paid rows. Orange identifies the explicit collection action. Preserve clear disabled and keyboard-focus states.
- Responsive: compact icon navigation below 800px; stacked performance facts, ledger, and collection console on phones. Tables scroll inside their container.
- No paper texture, Roman artwork, crayon borders, decorative sparkles, invented trust badges or unverified security claims. This visual redesign does not change credential storage, permissions or payment execution.
